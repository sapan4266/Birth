A few words from my heart

Dear favourite human,

Happy Birthday! ♡

I don't know if I say it often enough, but I am genuinely grateful to have you in my life. You are more than just a friend; you feel like family, like a sister, and like one of those rare people who make life feel a little softer and brighter.

Thank you for every laugh, every conversation, every bit of kindness and every memory. I hope you never forget how special you are and how much happiness you bring to the people around you.

On your birthday, I wish you endless reasons to smile, the courage to chase every dream, good health, peaceful days and a heart that always feels loved. May this next chapter bring you beautiful surprises and everything you truly deserve.

No matter how busy life gets or how far our paths travel, I will always be cheering for you. Keep smiling, keep shining and never change the beautiful person you are.

With lots of love,
Your forever well-wisher ♡
